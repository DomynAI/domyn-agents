Bank rules

Code the bank writes, not instructions to the model: each one rules on an action before it runs, from the bank's own data, and the agent cannot argue with it.

  • Blocking a cardalways allowed: it protects the customer, and can be undone.
  • Closing an alert as a false alarmallowed under a risk score of and asks an analyst from there on.
  • Releasing a held paymentalways asks an analyst, who can change the amount; more than the payment holds is refused, even if the analyst typed it.

New alert

Raised as the fraud engine would: the customer's data gets what the alert describes, and the agent takes it from the queue.
