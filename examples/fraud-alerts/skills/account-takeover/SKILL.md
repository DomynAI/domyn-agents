In an account takeover the fraudster has the customer's online banking credentials and
uses them to move money out, usually on the same day.

## Signs

The sequence matters more than any single event:

1. a password reset or a login from a country the customer is not in;
2. a new device registered;
3. a new payee added;
4. a large transfer to that payee, minutes later.

A new device alone is normal: customers change phones. A new payee alone is normal too.

## What to do

When the sequence is there, block the card: the fraudster may have the card details from
the same session. Never release a held transfer in an account takeover, whatever its
amount: the payment stays held until the customer is reached on a known phone number.
