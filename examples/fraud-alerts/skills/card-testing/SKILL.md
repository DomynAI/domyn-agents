name: card-testing
description: How to tell card testing from normal small purchases. Read it for alerts of type card_testing.
---

Card testing is a fraudster checking that a stolen card number works before using it for
something large. The card is usually not lost: the number leaked online.

## Signs

- Several online charges of 1 to 3 EUR in a few minutes, at unrelated merchants (gift
  cards, donations, game keys, vouchers).
- Merchants abroad, in a country the customer has not bought from in the last 90 days.
- A large online attempt right after the small ones, often declined by a limit.

One small charge, or small charges at a merchant the customer uses every month, is not
card testing.

## What to do

When the signs are there, block the card. The small charges are disputed for the customer
by the card team; you do not need to do anything about them. Do not close the alert as a
false alarm because the large attempt was declined: the next one may not be.
