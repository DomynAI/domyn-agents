# Code of conduct

Domyn Agents follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces is expected to follow it.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to the maintainers at [security@domyn.com](mailto:security@domyn.com). Reports are handled confidentially, and the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant decide the response.
